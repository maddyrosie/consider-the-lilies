Companionship services include emotional and social support, as well as assistance with day-to-day activities. The responsibilities of a companion are to accompany their clients through their daily routines, outings, hobbies, special occasions and beyond as a calm and supportive presence. 

Professional companionship is a service that can be tailored to the needs of an individual. Our services are provided exclusively in Winnipeg and are suitable for adults of any age. We conduct all of my services through a trauma informed and disability justice lens.

Connection is a fundamental part of the human experience. No matter one's life circumstances, we all deserve to feel connected and cared for.

Hourly rates for companionship start at $45, sliding scale and barter deals are available.

Companionship Services

  • Helping you or your loved one do light household chores, gardening and meal prep, etc.

  • Serenading you with a song on guitar

  • Regular visits to you or your loved one at a care facility

  • Accompanying you or your loved one to appointments

  • Advocacy

  • Regularly checking in and talking (in person, over text or on the phone)

  • Liaising with home care and other services to ensure quality care

  • And more! Contact us to discuss how we can meet your needs.
